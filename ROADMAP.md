# Roadmap

`shall-not-pass` is an over-engineered remote control for distractions. Here's where it could stand watch next. Ideas, not promises.

## ✅ Now

Spotify desktop app on macOS.

## 🔭 Next: your browser

A browser extension for YouTube, Spotify's web player, and other sites. One install covers Mac, Windows, and Linux, and only the interrupted tab goes quiet. ([#8](https://github.com/andrewgwoodruff/shall-not-pass/issues/8))

## 💡 Later

- **Windows:** the Spotify desktop app, muting only Spotify. ([#9](https://github.com/andrewgwoodruff/shall-not-pass/issues/9))
- **Android:** Spotify, with one catch: Android can't mute a single app, so all media goes quiet for the moment. ([#10](https://github.com/andrewgwoodruff/shall-not-pass/issues/10))
- **Mac, under the hood:** silence the app's audio directly instead of moving its volume slider. ([#11](https://github.com/andrewgwoodruff/shall-not-pass/issues/11))

## 🌄 Beyond the mountains, for now

iPhone and iPad apps, TV and console apps, and segments read by a podcast's host. Nothing outside those apps can see or control what they play today. If that changes, we'll find a way through.
